ExtraWork — Delete your data

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.1 · Android

Delete all local data

Settings has a permanent deletion control. It removes the local database, private job media, cached exports, stored approval links, the local account session, the analytics preference, and the diagnostic install identifier, then recreates an empty database containing only the built-in starter pricebook. It cannot be undone. Export first if you need to keep anything.

Delete everything by removing the app

Uninstalling ExtraWork removes its private database, its job media, and its keystore entries. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so there is no device-backup copy to clear afterwards.

Turn off analytics

Analytics is off unless you turned it on. Switching it off in Settings stops any further events, and costs you no functionality.

What deletion does not reach

An authorisation you have already sent to a customer — as a PDF, an email, or a message — is outside the app and must be deleted wherever it now lives. Deleting local data does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel that in Play.

What we hold

This release has no remote approval service configured, so there is no server-side copy of your authorisations, customers, or media. RevenueCat holds the anonymous purchase entitlement for your install. PostHog may hold opt-in analytics events containing no customer or job content. Sentry may hold a crash report with the error, the app version, and the device model.
