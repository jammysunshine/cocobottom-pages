ExtraWork — Support

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.1 · Android

Keep your records safe

ExtraWork keeps everything on this device, and Android's own backup is deliberately switched off for it. There is no account to restore from. Export your records — Settings can produce a versioned JSON copy including the photo bytes — before you replace the device, reset it, or remove the app.

Camera, photo, or microphone access is blocked

Open Android Settings, find ExtraWork, and allow the one you need. Camera and photos are only needed for job evidence; the microphone is only needed for voice drafting. Typing the scope by hand always works.

Voice drafting produces the wrong text

The transcript is a draft, not a record — edit it before you send anything. Speech recognition is performed by your device's own service, so its accuracy and language support come from Android, not from us.

Remote approval says it is unavailable

This release ships without the remote-approval service configured, so approval happens in person on the device. That message is expected behaviour, not a fault.

The customer will not approve on the spot

Save the authorisation as a draft and share the PDF. A frozen authorisation keeps its price and wording so you can send the same document again later.

Export or PDF will not generate

Free at least 100 MB of storage and retry. Large photo sets take longer; keep the app in the foreground while it renders.

Purchases

Prices and billing come from Google Play. Use "Restore purchases" while signed into the Play account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. See the purchase help page.
