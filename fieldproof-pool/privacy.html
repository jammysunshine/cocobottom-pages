FieldProof Pool — Privacy notice

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.2 · Android

FieldProof Pool records recurring pool-service visits: the steps completed, the readings you entered, the treatments you applied, photo evidence, exceptions, and a completion report for the customer. There is no FieldProof account, no server, and no automatic upload. Nothing you record leaves the device unless you deliberately share a report.

Stored on your device

Customer display names, site nicknames and addresses, water bodies and equipment, checklist templates, reading fields and the ranges you define, visit records, treatments, exceptions and their resolution notes, photographs, and generated reports are written to the app's private database and private file storage.

Site access notes — gate codes, key locations, and similar — are held in the Android keystore rather than in the database, and are marked as readable only on this device while it is unlocked. A copy of the database therefore carries the site and not the code. A per-install identifier is stored the same way.

Nothing is sent to us

This release contains no analytics, no crash reporting, and no diagnostic upload. There is no key for any such service in the build, so no event, report, or identifier is transmitted. If a future release adds any of them, this notice and the Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that release ships.

Purchases

Google Play processes payment for paid access. RevenueCat receives an anonymous installation identifier and the store entitlement state needed to unlock and restore that access. FieldProof Pool never receives your payment-card details.

Permissions

The camera is used only when you choose to capture service evidence. Photo-library access is used only when you deliberately select an existing image, which the report labels as imported. Biometric or device unlock is offered only to reveal a stored site access note. Each is requested at the moment you use it, and refusing any of them leaves the rest of the app usable.

Location

This release requests no location permission. Visit records carry the time you performed them, not a position.

Device backup

Android's own backup and device-transfer service is turned off for this app. Visit records, service photographs and access notes are not copied into the Google account backup of the device, and they do not travel to a new phone when you transfer one. That is deliberate — customer property details should not spread further than the device that took them. The consequence is that a lost or wiped device takes its records with it, so send the reports you need to keep.

Your controls

You can delete a site or a visit and its evidence, clear a stored access note, or remove the app to delete all of its local data. Deleted local data cannot be recovered by us. Reports you have already shared are controlled by whoever received them. Purchases stay with Google Play and can be restored.

Your customers' information

You decide what customer information to enter. You are the one responsible for having a proper basis to collect and keep it, and for answering any request a customer makes about it. We hold no copy and cannot answer such a request for you.

Children

FieldProof Pool is a tool for pool-service operators. It is not directed to children and collects nothing about them.
