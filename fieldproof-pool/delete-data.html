FieldProof Pool — Delete your data

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.2 · Android

Delete a visit or a site

Open the visit or the site and choose delete. Deleting a site removes its visits, readings, treatments, exceptions, photographs, and stored access note from this device.

Delete everything

Uninstalling FieldProof Pool removes its private database, its evidence files, and its keystore entries from the device.

What deletion does not reach

Any report you have already sent to a customer is outside the app and must be deleted wherever it now lives. There is no other copy to chase: Android's device backup is turned off for this app, so deleting here is the end of it. Deleting local data does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel that in Play.

There is nothing held on a server

FieldProof Pool has no account, no server database, and no analytics. RevenueCat holds only the anonymous purchase entitlement for your install, which exists to restore access you paid for. There is no record of your customers or sites for us to delete. If you would like written confirmation of that for a customer, email cocobottom2012@gmail.com.
