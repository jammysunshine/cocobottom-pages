FieldProof Pool — Support

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.2 · Android

Keep your records safe

FieldProof Pool keeps everything on this device. There is no account to restore from. Send the reports that matter before you replace the device, reset it, or remove the app.

Camera or photo access is blocked

Open Android Settings, find FieldProof Pool, and allow Camera. Photo-library access is only needed if you want to attach an existing image; capture works without it.

A stored access note will not open

Access notes are locked to this device and require the device to be unlocked. If biometric unlock fails, use the device passcode. A note stored on a previous device cannot be recovered on a new one — nothing in this app is backed up or transferred to another device.

A visit was interrupted

Reopen the app and continue. Work is saved after every interaction, so the visit resumes at the step you left.

A reading is flagged out of range

Ranges are the ones you entered for that site. Adjust them on the site's reading fields if the flag is wrong. The app compares numbers; it does not decide what is safe.

The report will not generate or send

Free at least 100 MB of storage and try again from the visit screen. Generation works offline; only the channel you share through may need a connection.

Purchases

Prices and billing come from Google Play. Use "Restore purchases" while signed into the Play account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. See the purchase help page.
