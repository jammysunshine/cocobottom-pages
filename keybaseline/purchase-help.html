KeyBaseline — Purchase help

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.1 · Android

Purchases belong to your Google account

There is no KeyBaseline account. Paid access is attached to the Google Play account that made the purchase, on this device.

Restore access

  1. Check the device is signed into the same Google account that bought it.
  2. Open the app's unlock or settings screen.
  3. Choose "Restore purchases".
  4. Stay online until the result appears.

Restoring never creates a charge.

Pending, cancelled, expired, or refunded

A pending Play transaction unlocks nothing until Play confirms it. Closing the purchase sheet creates no entitlement. If a subscription expires or a purchase is refunded, paid-only features stop at the next entitlement refresh. Handovers you have already signed and reports already generated stay readable — that is deliberate, because a sealed record is the thing you paid for.

Manage or cancel

Open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Cancelling happens at the Play level — removing the app or deleting local data does not cancel billing.

Charges and refunds

Google processes payment and refund requests under its own policies. We cannot see your card details and cannot reverse a Play charge directly. Check your Play purchase history first.
