KeyBaseline — Privacy notice

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.1 · Android

KeyBaseline records the condition of a property when keys change hands: a room-by-room condition sheet, inventory, meter readings, key counts, both parties' comments, and signatures, sealed into a report that the next handover can be compared against. There is no KeyBaseline account, no server database, and no automatic upload. Nothing you record leaves the device unless you deliberately share a report.

Stored on your device

Property labels and addresses, handover type and dates, party names, roles and optional contact details, signatures, room and element condition entries, inventory items with quantities and serial numbers, meter readings, key and access-card counts, comments, photographs and their SHA-256 checksums, and generated reports and addenda are written to the app's private database and private file storage.

This data is used only to complete the inspection, produce the report you ask for, and line a later handover up against an earlier one.

Crash reporting

This release sends crash and error reports to Sentry so that a failure can be diagnosed. The integration is configured with default personal-data collection switched off, so it does not attach your IP address or a user identity, and it carries no inspection content — no property address, party name, condition entry, comment, photograph, or file path. What it sends is the error itself plus the app version, the device model, and the Android version. Sentry processes this on our behalf under its own terms; data may be processed in the European Union.

Optional analytics

This release contains no product analytics. No usage events are collected.

Purchases

Google Play processes payment for paid access. RevenueCat receives an anonymous installation identifier and the store entitlement state needed to unlock and restore that access. KeyBaseline never receives your payment-card details.

Permissions

The camera is used only when you choose to attach condition evidence. Photo-library and document access are used only for files you deliberately select. Microphone access is blocked outright in this build. Each permission is requested at the moment you use the feature that needs it, and refusing any of them leaves the rest of the app usable.

Device backup

Android's own backup and device-transfer service is turned off for this app. Handover records and condition photographs are not copied into the Google account backup of the device, and they do not travel to a new phone when you transfer one. That is deliberate: a two-party condition record should stay on the device that made it. The consequence is that a lost or wiped device takes its records with it, so share the report with the other party while you still have it.

The other party's information

A handover record contains information about someone else — a tenant, a landlord, or an agent — including their name, possibly their contact details, their signature, and photographs of their home or belongings. Whoever organises the handover decides what to record and is responsible for having a proper basis to do so and for giving the other party a copy. Both parties are meant to receive the same sealed report. We hold no copy of it.

Your controls

You can delete an individual handover and its evidence, or remove the app to delete all of its local data. A signed report cannot be edited — a correction is a labelled addendum — but it can be deleted. Deleted local data cannot be recovered by us. Signed reports remain readable if paid access ends.

Children

KeyBaseline is a tool for tenants, landlords, and agents. It is not directed to children and collects nothing about them.
