KeyBaseline — Delete your data

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.1 · Android

Delete one handover

Open the handover and choose delete. This removes its condition entries, inventory, meter readings, key counts, comments, signatures, photographs, and generated reports from this device. A signed handover can be deleted even though it cannot be edited.

Delete everything

Uninstalling KeyBaseline removes its private database and all evidence files held on the device.

What deletion does not reach

A report you have already shared with the other party is outside the app and must be deleted wherever it now lives — and the other party has their own copy, which is the point of a two-party record. There is no other copy to chase: Android's device backup is turned off for this app, so deleting here is the end of it. Deleting local data does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel that in Play.

What we hold

KeyBaseline has no account and no server database, so there is no copy of your handovers for us to delete. Two services hold something narrow: RevenueCat holds the anonymous purchase entitlement for your install, which exists to restore access you paid for; and Sentry may hold a crash report from your device, containing the error, the app version, and the device model, with no inspection content and no identity attached.
