KeyBaseline — Support

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.1 · Android

Keep your records safe

KeyBaseline keeps everything on this device. There is no account to restore from. Share the signed report with the other party as soon as it is sealed, and keep your own copy somewhere outside the app before you replace the device, reset it, or remove KeyBaseline.

Camera or photo access is blocked

Open Android Settings, find KeyBaseline, and allow Camera. Photo and document access is only needed for files you choose yourself.

An inspection was interrupted

Reopen the app and continue. Work is autosaved, so the handover resumes where you left it.

The other party is not present, or will not sign

Record it. The app has an explicit absent and declined path, so a missing signature is never shown as agreement. The report still seals and can still be shared.

A move-out comparison shows the wrong items

Link the new handover to the prior one on the setup screen. Items that do not match are left as new or removed rather than guessed at; you can map them yourself on the comparison screen.

The report will not generate

Free at least 100 MB of storage and try again. Large photo appendices take longer; keep the app in the foreground while it renders.

I need to correct a signed report

Add an addendum. A signed revision cannot be edited by design — that is what makes it worth anything to either party — but an addendum sits alongside it and is visible to both.

Purchases

Prices and billing come from Google Play. Use "Restore purchases" while signed into the Play account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. See the purchase help page.
