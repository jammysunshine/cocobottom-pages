WarrantyWallet Privacy Notice

Effective 13 August 2026

WarrantyWallet is local-first and does not require an account. Purchase records, receipts, product evidence, claims, coverage, reminders, and service history stay in the app-private storage on the user's device unless the user chooses to share an export.

Processing and services

Receipt text extraction runs on device. Optional anonymous analytics are disabled by default and contain only allowlisted workflow events and counts, never record content. Release builds may send scrubbed crash diagnostics with personal, request, breadcrumb, context, extra, and raw exception details removed. Apple or Google processes payments; RevenueCat manages entitlement state.

Your control

Settings can export readable JSON/CSV data with evidence or permanently delete local records, evidence, reminders, consent, and the diagnostic ID. Uninstalling without an export removes local content. Contact the support address in the store listing for privacy questions.
