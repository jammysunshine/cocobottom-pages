Delete Your WarrantyWallet Data

Effective 22 August 2026

WarrantyWallet keeps no account and no server-side copy of your records. Purchase records, receipts, product evidence, claims, coverage, reminders and service history exist only in app-private storage on your device, so deletion happens on the device and takes effect immediately.

Delete everything

Open Settings → Delete all data and confirm. This permanently removes local records, evidence files, reminders, stored consent, and the random diagnostic ID. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want to keep a copy.

Export before deleting

Use Settings → Export to write readable JSON or CSV with the evidence attached. The export is written where you choose and is yours to keep or destroy.

Delete a single item

Open the item and use its delete action. Its evidence files are removed with it.

Uninstalling

Removing the app deletes its private storage, subject to your operating system's backup behaviour. If you have previously exported or shared a file, that copy is outside the app and must be deleted separately.

Optional analytics

Analytics is off unless you turned it on, and carries no record content — only allowlisted workflow events and counts. Turning it off in Settings stops any further events. To have previously collected anonymous events removed, email the address below.

Purchases

Entitlement state is held by Apple or Google and RevenueCat against an anonymous installation identifier, and is not deleted by the in-app control. Manage or delete it through your store account.

Requesting deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, email the support address below from any address and say which platform you used. Because there is no account and no server copy, there is nothing held remotely to erase beyond the anonymous items named above.
