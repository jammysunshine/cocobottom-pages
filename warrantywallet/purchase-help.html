WarrantyWallet Purchase Help

Effective 22 August 2026

WarrantyWallet Plus is sold through the app store on your device. Apple or Google takes the payment; RevenueCat records which entitlement your installation holds. WarrantyWallet never sees or stores a card number.

I paid but Plus is not unlocked

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while the device is online and signed in to the same store account that made the purchase. Entitlements are tied to that store account, not to the device.

The paywall shows nothing to buy

This usually means the store could not return the product list — a network problem, a store account signed out, or a region where the product is not offered. Close and reopen the app while online. If it persists, email the support address below with your platform and app version.

Refunds and cancellation

Refunds, payment methods, invoices and cancellation are handled entirely by Apple or Google. WarrantyWallet cannot issue a refund or cancel a purchase on your behalf. Use your store account's purchase history.

Reinstalling or changing devices

Plus follows your store account, so Restore Purchases re-enables it on a new device. Your warranty records do not — they are stored only on the device. Use Settings → Export before you reinstall or switch devices.
