ExpiryGuard Terms of Use

Effective 19 August 2026

ExpiryGuard is a reminder and organisation tool for documents you already hold. It does not renew anything for you, does not submit applications, and is not affiliated with any government department, issuing authority, or insurer. Always check renewal requirements and deadlines against the authority that issued the document.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your device. Delivery depends on Android and on your notification settings, and can be delayed or suppressed by battery optimisation, do-not-disturb, or a device restart. Treat them as a helpful prompt, not as a guarantee that you will be told in time.

Purchases

Google Play bills the Pro upgrade. A subscription renews until you cancel it in your Google Play account; cancelling stops future charges and leaves access running to the end of the paid period. Eligible purchases can be restored in the app.

Your records

Your records stay on your device, so keeping the device secure and keeping your own backup — by exporting — is yours to manage. Deleting the app or its data removes those records permanently.

Availability

The app is provided as it is and as available. Nothing here limits consumer rights you have under the law where you live.
