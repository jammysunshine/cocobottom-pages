Common questions

No reminders are arriving

Open your device's notification settings and allow notifications for ExpiryGuard, then open the document, edit it and save it again. Saving is what schedules the alerts. Your reminder dates stay visible in the app even while permission is refused, so nothing is lost by having said no earlier.

Scanning read the wrong details

Scanning only proposes values for you to confirm — nothing is saved until you accept it. Correct any field on the confirmation screen, or type the document in by hand. Manual entry always works and is never gated.

I paid for Pro but do not have it

Use Restore purchases in Settings while signed in to the same Google account that made the purchase.

I cannot get past the app lock

Retry the biometric unlock, or use your device passcode when it is offered. A failed unlock never erases anything.

I need a copy of my records

Open Export in Settings, choose the format, decide whether reference numbers and images should be included, then hand the file to the app you want through the Android share sheet.

What we will never ask you for

Support will ask for your app version, Android version, device model, what you were doing, roughly when, and whether the device was online. Support will never ask you to send a document image, a reference number, your exported inventory, or a recording of your screen. Nobody at ExpiryGuard can see your records — they are on your device only.

Deleting your data

Settings has a Delete all data action that empties every record, attachment, reminder, and consent setting on the device. See deleting your data for detail.
