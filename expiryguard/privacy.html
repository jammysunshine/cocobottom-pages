ExpiryGuard Privacy Notice

Effective 19 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

ExpiryGuard organises renewal dates for documents such as passports, licences, and insurance policies. It has no ExpiryGuard account, no server database, and no automatic document upload. Nothing you record is sent anywhere unless you deliberately export it.

Stored on your device

Holder labels, document metadata, expiry dates, renewal notes, reminder plans, checklists, OCR confirmation records, and drafts are written to the app's private SQLite database. Imported images and PDFs are copied into the app's protected document directory. A document's reference number — a passport or policy number — is written to the Android keystore rather than to the database, so a copy of the database carries the label and not the number.

This data is used only to show the renewal timeline, schedule local notifications, keep renewal history, and build an export you ask for.

Backup and device transfer

Operating-system backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for ExpiryGuard's data, so records and protected attachments stay in the app sandbox. An export you create and share through the Android share sheet is outside that boundary — the recipient you choose controls that copy.

Crash reporting

This release ships with crash reporting switched off. No diagnostic reporting key is configured in the build, so no crash report leaves the device. If a future release enables it, this notice and the Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that release ships.

Optional analytics

Anonymous product analytics is off until you switch it on in Settings, and consent is not assumed at first launch. When it is on, the app can send only a fixed list of outcome event names and three coarse properties: the platform, whether an action succeeded, was cancelled, or failed, and a bucketed count. It cannot send free text. Holder names, document labels, issuers, reference numbers, expiry dates, notes, images, filenames, or exports are never sent. Analytics is processed by PostHog over HTTPS.

Purchases

Google Play processes payment for the Pro upgrade. RevenueCat receives the store entitlement state needed to unlock and restore Pro. ExpiryGuard never receives payment-card details.

Permissions

Notifications, camera, photos, and biometric unlock are each optional and are requested only at the moment you use the feature that needs them. Refusing any of them leaves the rest of the app fully usable; manual entry is always available.

Your controls

You can archive or permanently delete an individual record, export a redacted or expanded inventory, switch analytics off, or delete all local app data from Settings. Deleted local data cannot be recovered by ExpiryGuard. Purchases remain with Google Play and can be restored.

Children

ExpiryGuard is a household utility rated for adults. It is not directed to children and collects nothing about them.
