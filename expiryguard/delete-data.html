Deleting your ExpiryGuard data

ExpiryGuard keeps your records on your device. There is no account to close and no server copy to request, so deletion is something you carry out yourself and it takes effect immediately.

Delete everything

Open Settings and choose Delete all local data, then confirm. This empties the document records, holders, reminder plans, checklists, drafts, and the analytics consent setting, removes imported images and PDFs from the app's protected directory, and clears reference numbers from the Android keystore. Scheduled notifications are cancelled. The app returns to its first-run state.

This cannot be undone, and ExpiryGuard cannot recover it for you. Export first if you want a copy.

Delete one record

Open the document and choose Delete. Archiving instead keeps the record readable while freeing its slot.

Uninstalling

Removing the app from your device also removes everything it stored.

Analytics

Anonymous analytics is off unless you switched it on, and it never carries your document content — only outcome event names and coarse counts.
