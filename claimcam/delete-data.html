Delete ClaimCam data

ClaimCam has no user account. You can remove local incident records yourself and request deletion of limited processor-held identifiers.

DELETE ONE INCIDENT

  1. Open ClaimCam and choose Archive.
  2. Open the incident you want to remove.
  3. Choose Delete incident, then confirm.

This deletes the incident database record, its photos stored by ClaimCam, and its generated reports from the app's private storage.

DELETE ALL LOCAL APP DATA

  1. Open Settings & privacy.
  2. Choose Delete all local app data.
  3. Choose Delete local data to confirm.

This deletes the Glovebox, incident records, stored evidence files, generated reports, local preferences, the installation identifier, and the prior anonymous analytics identifier. Uninstalling ClaimCam also removes its app-private local data.

REQUEST PROCESSOR-HELD DATA DELETION

In ClaimCam, open Settings & privacy → Support and recovery and choose Request provider-data deletion. This prepares an email containing anonymous support identifiers, without incident evidence. You may also email cocobottom2012@gmail.com directly.

Verified requests delete eligible ClaimCam analytics and purchase-entitlement records held for the developer by PostHog or RevenueCat, normally within 30 days. We may ask for the in-app support identifiers so the request can be matched without an account.

DATA THAT IS KEPT ELSEWHERE

  • PDFs, photos, or other copies you exported are controlled by the destination you selected and must be deleted there.
  • Google Play controls store account, payment, and purchase-history records. Google or the developer's processors may retain limited transaction, fraud-prevention, security, tax, or legal records for the period required by law or platform rules.
  • ClaimCam support cannot retrieve or delete incident evidence because the developer never receives a cloud copy.
