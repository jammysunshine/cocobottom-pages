ClaimCam privacy notice

ClaimCam is designed to keep incident evidence under your control.

WHAT CLAIMCAM STORES

Your incident data stays on this device.

Vehicle details, people and witness details, factual notes, photos, optional coordinates, checksums, drafts, and generated report records are stored in ClaimCam's private app storage. ClaimCam does not provide an account, cloud sync, insurer upload, or developer-accessible backup for this information. ClaimCam preserves selected photo files; those originals may already contain camera metadata written by the device that created them.

WHEN DATA LEAVES THE DEVICE

Only when you choose an action.

A generated PDF leaves ClaimCam only when you use the operating system share sheet or save it somewhere you select. The receiving app or service then applies its own privacy practices. Store purchases are processed by Apple or Google and RevenueCat; ClaimCam receives entitlement status and transaction metadata, not payment-card details.

OPTIONAL DIAGNOSTICS

Both controls are off by default.

If you enable anonymous usage analytics, ClaimCam sends only a restricted set of coarse workflow events to PostHog. If you enable crash reporting, ClaimCam may send stripped crash diagnostics to Sentry. Neither option permits incident text, names, policy references, photos, coordinates, report contents, screenshots, view hierarchies, advertising identifiers, or device serials. You can turn either option off in Settings at any time.

PERMISSIONS

Contextual and optional.

Camera access is requested when you choose to take an evidence photo. Photo-library access is requested when you choose to import. Location is requested only when you choose to attach a location snapshot. Denying any of these permissions does not prevent manual documentation or honest unavailable-field markers.

RETENTION AND DELETION

You control retention.

Incident records remain until you delete an individual record, delete all local app data in Settings, or uninstall the app. Delete all also removes local preferences and replaces any prior anonymous analytics identifier. Deletion removes ClaimCam's local copies; reports previously saved or shared elsewhere must be deleted from those locations separately. Store purchase records remain with Apple or Google so purchases can be restored.

See the ClaimCam data deletion instructions to remove local records or request deletion of processor-held data.

CHILDREN AND CHANGES

ClaimCam is not directed to children under 13. Material changes to this notice will be identified by a new effective date in the app and on this page.
