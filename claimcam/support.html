ClaimCam support

Troubleshooting that keeps your incident evidence under your control.

Your evidence is not available to support.

ClaimCam has no account or developer-accessible cloud copy of your records. Support cannot retrieve deleted data, open your local incident records, or resend a report.

COMMON RECOVERY STEPS

A draft did not appear

Reopen ClaimCam on the same device and installation. Do not clear app data or uninstall before checking the Home and Archive screens.

Camera, photos, or location are unavailable

Continue with manual notes or mark the item unavailable. You may enable the permission later in the device's system settings; ClaimCam never requires location to finish a record.

A PDF will not share

Confirm the device has free space, generate the PDF again, and select a share destination that accepts PDF files. The incident record remains readable even if sharing fails.

A purchase is missing

Open ClaimCam Plus and choose Restore purchases while signed into the same Apple or Google store account. Restoring access does not alter local incident data.
