SnagList — Privacy notice

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

SnagList records property handover defects room by room — descriptions, photographs, annotations, floor plans — and turns them into a report. It keeps that content on your device. There is no SnagList account, no cloud project database, and no automatic upload.

Stored on your device

Property names and addresses, room names, issue descriptions and notes, floor plans, photographs, annotations, reports, and local exports are written to the app's private storage. Content leaves the app only when you choose to share or export it.

Optional analytics and diagnostics

Analytics and crash reporting are off until you switch on "Share anonymous product signals" in Settings. When it is on, PostHog may receive allowlisted event names, counts, broad categories, the report type, and technical device and app information. Crash reporting is built in but is not active in the released build: no Sentry destination is configured, so turning the switch on sends no crash data anywhere. If that changes, this page and the Play Data safety declaration will say so before the build ships. The app forbids sending addresses, property names, issue text, notes, room labels, photographs, plans, filenames, file paths, request data, user identity, or breadcrumbs through those adapters. You can withdraw consent in Settings at any time, which stops any further collection.

Purchases

Google Play processes payment. RevenueCat receives an anonymous installation identifier and the store transaction and entitlement information the app needs to show products, unlock access, and restore purchases. SnagList never receives your payment-card details.

Permissions

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a defect. Photo and document access are used only when you choose an existing image or floor plan. Sharing happens only when you ask to export or share. You can change any of these in Android Settings.

Device backup

Android's own backup service is switched off for this app, so inspection content stays in the app sandbox rather than being copied into a Google account backup. That also means a lost or reset device takes unexported content with it — export the reports you need to keep.

Retention, export, and deletion

Local content stays until you delete a property, use "Delete all local data", or remove the app. Settings can export the database records as JSON. Photographs and generated PDFs are separate files and can be shared individually. Deleting local data does not cancel a Google Play subscription, and does not erase records that Google, RevenueCat, PostHog, or Sentry retain under their own policies.

Security

Inspection content uses application-private storage and Android's own platform protections. No system is perfectly secure, and device loss, app removal, or storage failure can make unexported content unrecoverable.

Children and international processing

SnagList is a property-inspection tool and is not directed to children. Google, RevenueCat, PostHog, and Sentry may process data in countries other than yours under their published safeguards.
