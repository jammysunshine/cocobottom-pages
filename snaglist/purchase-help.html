SnagList — Purchase help

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Purchases belong to your Google account

There is no SnagList account. Paid access is attached to the Google Play account that made the purchase, on this device.

Products

Homebuyer is a one-time purchase intended to unlock unlimited properties and issues plus full inspection and reinspection PDF export. Inspector Pro is an annual subscription intended to add report branding, reusable room defaults, and CSV export. The app shows the product title and localised price supplied by Google Play; the Play purchase confirmation is authoritative for the billing period, taxes, renewal terms, and the final charge.

Restore access

  1. Check the device is signed into the same Google account that made the purchase.
  2. Open SnagList Settings or the unlock screen.
  3. Choose "Restore purchases".
  4. Stay online until the result appears.

Restoring never creates a charge. Family sharing depends on how the Play product is configured and is not promised unless the Play listing says so.

Pending, cancelled, expired, or refunded

A pending Play transaction unlocks nothing until Play confirms it. Closing the purchase sheet creates no entitlement. When a subscription expires or a purchase is refunded or revoked, new paid exports and paid-only features stop after the next entitlement refresh. Reports already generated and stored locally stay readable.

Manage or cancel

Open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Cancelling happens at the Play level — removing SnagList or deleting local data does not cancel billing.

Charges and refunds

Google processes payment and refund requests under its own policies. We cannot see your full card information and cannot reverse a Play charge directly. Check your Play purchase history before contacting support.
