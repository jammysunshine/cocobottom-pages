SnagList — Delete your data

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Delete one property

Open the inspection from your inspections list, scroll to Delete inspection, and tap Delete this inspection. That removes its rooms, issues, notes, annotations, photographs, and reports from this device. There is no delete control on the inspections list itself.

Delete all local data

Settings has a "Delete all local data" control that permanently removes the app's database and private files after a confirmation. It cannot be undone. Export what you need first — Settings can produce a JSON export of the database records, and reports and photographs can be shared individually.

Delete everything by removing the app

Uninstalling SnagList removes its private database and files. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so there is no device-backup copy to clear afterwards.

Turn off analytics and diagnostics

Both are off unless you switched on "Share anonymous product signals". Switching it off in Settings stops any further collection.

What deletion does not reach

A report you have already shared is outside the app and must be deleted wherever it now lives. Deleting local data does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel that in Play.

What we hold

There is no account and no cloud project database, so there is no copy of your inspections for us to delete. RevenueCat holds the anonymous purchase entitlement for your install. If you opted in, PostHog may hold allowlisted analytics events and Sentry may hold scrubbed crash diagnostics, neither containing inspection content.
