SnagList — Support

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Keep your work safe

SnagList stores inspections on this device, and Android's own backup is deliberately switched off for it. There is no account to restore from. Export the reports and records that matter before you remove the app, replace the device, or reset it.

Camera or photo access is blocked

Open Android Settings, find SnagList, and allow Camera or Photos. If access stays unavailable, continue the draft and add evidence later — but note that SnagList requires evidence before an issue can be saved.

A report will not generate

Keep the app in the foreground while a large report renders, make sure at least 100 MB of storage is free, and retry from the report screen. If a local image is missing, the report names it rather than quietly leaving it out.

Reports after a subscription ends

Saved reports stay listed and readable when paid access ends.

Export or delete your data

Settings can export the local database records as JSON and can permanently delete all local app data after a confirmation. Photographs and PDFs are separate private files — share the reports you need before deleting. See the deletion page.

Purchases

Prices and billing come from Google Play. Use "Restore purchases" while signed into the Play account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel an Inspector Pro subscription in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. See the purchase help page.
