JobProof — Privacy notice

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.2 · Android

JobProof records the condition of a job site before work begins: guided photos, an indexed list of pre-existing damage, and a customer acknowledgement, sealed into a PDF evidence pack. There is no JobProof account, no server, and no automatic upload. Nothing you record leaves the device unless you deliberately share an export.

Stored on your device

Customer labels and references, service addresses, trade template selections, capture prompts, photographs and their SHA-256 checksums, defect entries, acknowledgement records and signatures, omission reasons, and generated evidence packs are written to the app's private database and private file storage. A per-install identifier used to distinguish one device's records is held in the Android keystore rather than in the database.

This data is used only to run the capture protocol, build the evidence pack you ask for, and let you find a sealed record again during a dispute.

Capture context

Each photograph is stored with the time it was taken, the device time zone, the app and device version, and — only when you have enabled location capture and granted the permission — coarse coordinates and the accuracy the device reported. When location is denied or unavailable the record says so rather than leaving a gap. Location is context on the record; it is never used to infer that a job was completed, and it is never sent anywhere.

Nothing is sent to us

This release contains no analytics, no crash reporting, and no diagnostic upload. There is no key for any such service in the build, so no event, report, or identifier is transmitted. If a future release adds any of them, this notice and the Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that release ships.

Purchases

This release has no in-app purchase and no payment code of any kind.

Permissions

The camera is used only inside the capture workflow, and only after you grant it. Photo-library access is used only when you deliberately add a supplementary image, which is labelled as imported in the report. Location is optional and off until you enable it. Refusing any permission leaves the rest of the app usable.

Device backup

Android's own backup and device-transfer service is turned off for this app. Job records and evidence photographs are not copied into the Google account backup of the device, and they do not travel to a new phone when you transfer one. That is deliberate: it keeps the evidence where it was captured. The consequence is that a lost or wiped device takes its records with it, so export the packs you need to keep.

Your controls

You can delete an individual job record and its captures, or remove the app to delete all of its local data. A sealed record cannot be edited — corrections are appended as a supplement — but it can be deleted. Deleted local data cannot be recovered by us. Export what you need to keep before deleting anything.

Children

JobProof is a tool for tradespeople. It is not directed to children and collects nothing about them.
