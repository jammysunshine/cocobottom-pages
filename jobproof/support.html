JobProof — Support

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.2 · Android

Keep your records safe

JobProof keeps everything on this device. There is no account to restore from. Share or save the evidence packs that matter before you replace the device, reset it, or remove the app.

Camera or photo access is blocked

Open Android Settings, find JobProof, and allow Camera. Photo-library access is only needed if you want to attach a supplementary image; the guided capture works without it.

Location says unavailable

Location is optional. If you want it on the record, enable location capture in the app and grant the permission, and make sure device location services are on. When it is unavailable the record states that plainly instead of guessing — that is intended behaviour, not a fault.

A capture was interrupted

Reopen the app and continue the job. Work is saved after each step, so completed prompts are retained.

The evidence pack will not generate

Free at least 100 MB of storage and try again from the job screen. Large packs take longer; keep the app in the foreground while it renders.

The customer will not sign

Record the refusal. The app has an explicit "declined" and "not present" path so that a missing signature is never presented as an approval.
