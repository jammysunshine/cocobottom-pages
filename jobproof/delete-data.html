JobProof — Delete your data

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 0.2 · Android

Delete one job

Open the job from the Jobs list and choose delete. This removes the job record, its captures, its defect entries, and the acknowledgement from this device. Sealed records can be deleted even though they cannot be edited.

Delete everything

Uninstalling JobProof removes its private database and all evidence files held on the device.

What deletion does not reach

Any evidence pack you have already shared — by email, message, or a file saved elsewhere — is outside the app and must be deleted where it now lives. There is no other copy to chase: Android's device backup is turned off for this app, so deleting here is the end of it.

There is nothing held on a server

JobProof has no account, no server database, and no analytics. There is no remote copy of your records for us to delete, and no request you need to make to us. If you would like written confirmation of that for a customer or insurer, email cocobottom2012@gmail.com.
