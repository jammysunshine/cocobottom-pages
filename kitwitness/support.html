KitWitness — Support

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Keep your records safe

KitWitness keeps everything on this device. There is no account to restore from. Share or save the sealed reports that matter before you replace the device, reset it, or remove the app.

Camera access is blocked

Open Android Settings, find KitWitness, and allow Camera. If you cannot, use "Choose existing photo" or enter the kit code by hand.

A capture was interrupted

Reopen the app and use Resume capture. Steps you had already committed are retained.

Low storage

Free at least 100 MB and retry the current screen. PDF generation and photo capture both need room to work.

Sharing is unavailable

The sealed record stays on the device. Retry on a device or share target that supports it — nothing is lost by a failed share.

The wrong kit is open at return

Stop and open the matching kit label or code. Never move evidence between kit records; a record attached to the wrong item is not usable.

A purchase is missing

Use "Restore purchases" while online and signed into the Play account that bought it. Manage or cancel in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Sending us a diagnostic

The Settings screen can copy a diagnostic payload that contains only the app version, the platform, a random diagnostic identifier, and the time it was collected. There is no customer or kit content in it. Paste that into your email to us.

Contact

Email cocobottom2012@gmail.com with the app version, your device model, the Android version, the visible error text, which step you were on, and whether the device was offline. Please do not send condition photographs, customer contact details, or an unredacted report unless it is genuinely essential.
