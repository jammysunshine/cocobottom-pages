What this app is

KitWitness helps an equipment-rental operator record the condition of a kit at hand-out and at return, and capture the customer's acknowledgement of it. You must be legally able to accept these terms and use the app only where permitted.

It does not inspect equipment, assess whether damage is fair wear, value a repair, decide who is liable, or guarantee that a record will be accepted by a customer, an insurer, or a court. A dispute over condition or liability stays between you and your customer.

Match the record to the kit
