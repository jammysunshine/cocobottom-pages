KitWitness — Delete your data

Effective 22 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Delete one handback

Open the handback and choose delete, then confirm. This removes its database rows, captures, signatures, addenda, and report PDFs from the device. The erasure is queued durably before the record is removed, so if it is interrupted the app finishes it the next time you open it.

Delete everything

Uninstalling KitWitness removes its private database and all evidence files held on the device.

Turn off analytics

Analytics is off unless you turned it on. Switching it off in Settings stops any further events. Because the app only ever sends event names, a result, and a bucketed count, there is no personal record held at PostHog to retrieve — but you can ask us to delete the analytics profile for your install by email.

What deletion does not reach

A report you have already shared with a customer is outside the app and must be deleted wherever it now lives. There is no other copy to chase: Android's device backup is turned off for this app, so deleting here is the end of it. Deleting local data does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel that in Play.

What we hold

There is no account and no server database of your kits or customers. RevenueCat holds the anonymous purchase entitlement for your install. PostHog may hold opt-in analytics events with no content in them. Sentry may hold a crash report with the error, the app version, and the device model and no kit or customer content.
