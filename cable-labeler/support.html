Cable Labeler — Support

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

What support can and cannot do

The app says it on the Settings & privacy screen: "Support can answer questions about how the app works, but cannot see, recover or delete your cables — those never leave this device." There is no account and no server copy, so we cannot look up your catalogue, restore it, or delete it for you.

Keep your work safe

Cable Labeler stores your catalogue on this device only, and Android's own backup is deliberately switched off for it. A lost, reset or replaced device takes the catalogue with it, and there is nothing to restore from. Treat the device as the only copy.

The app does not talk to a printer

Cable Labeler does not control a printer and never contacts one. "Record reprint locally" saves a new immutable entry in that cable's local label history, and "Mark this label printed locally" updates that history only. Printing the physical sticker is something you do outside the app.

Finding a cable

Open Search cables on the Cable library screen. As the app puts it, search covers the cable name, endpoints, identifier and note; it also matches the connector, the role, the location path and the status, and typing a code exactly still finds a cable you have marked disposed. Searching keeps working on every tier — it is never gated behind Pro, and it keeps working if the store is unreachable or a purchase is cancelled.

"The catalogue is unavailable"

If the library reports that local storage cannot be read, use Try opening the catalogue again or Try reading the catalogue again on the same screen. While storage is unavailable the app will not let you edit or delete anything, and the diagnostics answer cannot be changed either, so nothing is sent.

The 21st cable is a locked draft

Free holds 20 active cables, one location list and one printable sheet a month. Past that, the next cable is saved as a locked draft: it is still catalogued and still findable, it just cannot print. Unlocking Pro releases it in place, keeping the same cable and the same identifier code, so a label already printed for it stays correct.

Diagnostics

The single switch Send anonymous diagnostics in Settings & privacy controls both product analytics and crash reporting. It is off unless you turn it on, and the line beside it always tells you which way it is set. No analytics key is configured in this release, so in this build no analytics events are sent in either position.

Delete your data

Use Delete cable… on a single cable, or Erase everything in Settings & privacy for the whole catalogue; uninstalling also removes it. All three are irreversible and there is no copy to restore from. Deleting a single cable deliberately keeps its label-history entries and revokes rather than erases its codes; only Erase everything drops those too. See the deletion page.

Purchases

Cable Labeler Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. Prices and billing come from Google Play. Use Restore purchases on the Cable Labeler Pro screen while signed into the Play account that made the purchase. See the purchase help page.
