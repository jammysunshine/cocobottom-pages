Cable Labeler — Terms of use

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

What this app is

Cable Labeler keeps a private, on-device catalogue of physical cables — a name, the two ends a cable runs between, an optional identifier code and a note — so you can search that catalogue to identify a real cable, and record when a label was generated and applied. You must be legally able to accept these terms and use the app only where permitted.

It is a record, not a printer and not a certification

Cable Labeler does not control a printer, does not connect to one, and does not print anything. Recording a reprint or marking a label printed updates that cable's local label history only; producing and applying the physical sticker is entirely yours. The app provides no electrical, network, data-centre, structural, fire-safety, code-compliance, legal or other professional advice. A label, a code or a route recorded in the app is your own record, not a verification that a cable is safe, correct, de-energised or fit to move. Never rely on the app in place of physically tracing, testing and isolating a cable, or in place of a qualified professional. You are responsible for what you catalogue, how you label it, and what you do on the strength of it.

Local data is your responsibility

There is no account, no server and no developer-operated backup, and Android's own backup is deliberately switched off for this app. Keeping the device and preserving access to it are yours to manage. Deletion — one cable, "Erase everything", or uninstalling — is irreversible, and because nothing was ever uploaded there is no copy to restore from. Labels already applied to real cables will scan as unavailable after an erase.

Free and paid access

The free tier holds 20 active cables, one location list and one printable sheet a month; beyond that the next cable is kept as a locked draft that remains findable but cannot print. Cable Labeler Pro is a one-time purchase and not a subscription: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. Pro is intended to add unlimited active cables, bulk create and edit, CSV import and export, custom label templates, saved filter reports, relabel tools, and history for disposed cables. The current product, price and taxes are supplied by Google Play before you purchase, and the Play confirmation is authoritative. Restoring purchases uses the Play account currently signed in on the device. Searching and code lookup are never withheld by tier, cancellation or store availability, and no purchase outcome — including a cancellation or an offline store — changes catalogue data.
