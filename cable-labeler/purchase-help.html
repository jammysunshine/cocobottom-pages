Cable Labeler — Purchase help

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Purchases belong to your Google account

There is no Cable Labeler account. Paid access is attached to the Google Play account that made the purchase, on this device.

The product

One thing is for sale: Cable Labeler Pro. The paywall states it plainly — "A one-time purchase. There is no subscription." — and shows the price as "{price} — paid once." Nothing here renews and there is nothing to cancel. The app shows the product title and localised price supplied by Google Play; the Play purchase confirmation is authoritative for taxes and the final charge. Reach the screen with See what Pro adds from the Cable library.

What Free keeps, and what Pro adds

Free holds 20 active cables, one location list, and one printable sheet a month. When you go past the free cable limit, the next cable is kept as a locked draft: it is still in your catalogue and still findable by search, it just cannot print until Pro is unlocked. Searching never stops working, whatever tier you are on and whatever happens with the store. (The paywall words that promise as "Searching and scanning never stop working"; this release ships no code scanner, so in practice it is search and manual code lookup that are never gated.)

Pro is described on screen as adding unlimited active cables, bulk create and edit, CSV import and export, custom label templates, saved filter reports, relabel tools, and history for cables you have disposed of. When Pro becomes active, locked drafts are unlocked in place — the same cable, keeping its existing identifier code, so a label you already printed stays correct.

Buy

Open Cable Labeler Pro and tap Buy Pro for {price}, with the price the store supplies. If the screen instead says "The store is not available in this build" or "The store has no Pro product right now", nothing can be bought at that moment; your catalogue is unchanged and keeps working either way.

Restore access

  1. Check the device is signed into the same Google account that made the purchase.
  2. Open Cable Labeler Pro from "See what Pro adds".
  3. Choose Restore purchases.
  4. Stay online until the result appears.

Restoring never creates a charge. Family sharing depends on how the Play product is configured and is not promised unless the Play listing says so.

Pending, cancelled, offline, or refunded

A pending Play transaction unlocks nothing until Play confirms it, and closing the purchase sheet creates no entitlement. A cancelled purchase, an unreachable store or a failed transaction is never allowed to touch your catalogue — no cable, code, print job or note changes because of a purchase outcome. If a purchase is later refunded or revoked, Pro-only features stop after the next entitlement check, but every cable you catalogued stays on the device and stays searchable.

Charges and refunds

Google processes payment and refund requests under its own policies. We cannot see your full card information and cannot reverse a Play charge directly. Check your Play purchase history before contacting support.

Erasing your data does not affect your purchase

"Erase everything" in Settings & privacy deliberately keeps the Pro entitlement, because a purchase belongs to your store account and not to the catalogue. Removing the app does not refund anything either; use Restore purchases after reinstalling.
