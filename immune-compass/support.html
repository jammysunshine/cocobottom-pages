Immune Compass — Support

Effective 19 October 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Not medical advice

Immune Compass helps you keep and organise your own health record. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Talk to your clinician about any change in your health, and contact your local emergency services in an emergency. We cannot answer medical questions by email.

Keep your records safe

Immune Compass stores your records only on this device. Android's cloud backup is deliberately switched off for it, and there is no account to restore from. Removing the app, resetting the phone or moving to a new one leaves the record behind.

The app's workspace backup file is encrypted with a key that exists only in this installation, so it cannot be opened on another phone or after reinstalling. It is not a way to keep your records. Before you remove the app, replace the device or reset it, save or share a visit pack as a PDF with the entries you want to keep.

Camera or photo access is blocked

Open Android Settings, find Immune Compass under Apps, then Permissions, and allow Camera. If Android also asks for access to your pictures, allow it as well. Depending on your Android version this is called Photos and videos, or Files and media. If you previously chose "Don't allow", Android will not ask again until you change it in Settings.

Reminders do not appear

Allow notifications for Immune Compass in Android Settings. Reminders are scheduled on the device, so a battery-saving setting that stops the app can delay them; exempting Immune Compass from battery optimisation helps. Do not rely on the app as the only reminder for a time-critical medicine.

Export or erase your data

The app can prepare a visit pack, which you save as a PDF or share through Android's share sheet. Erasing your saved workspace asks you to confirm before anything is removed, and it applies to the current workspace. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. See the deletion page for the steps.
