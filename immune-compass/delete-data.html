Immune Compass — Delete your data

Effective 19 October 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Immune Compass keeps no account and no server-side copy of your records. Your symptoms, medications, lab results, photographs, documents, reminders and visit packs exist only in the app's private storage on your device, so deletion happens on the device and takes effect there.

Export first

Erasing cannot be undone. If you want to keep a copy, use the app's export to create a file and save or share it through Android's share sheet before you erase. That file is yours to keep or destroy.

Erase in the app

Open Settings → My saved workspace, choose Erase this workspace…, and then confirm the erase. This permanently removes the workspace's records and private files from this device. If a private file cannot be removed straight away, the app queues its clean-up and retries automatically.

Delete everything by removing the app

Uninstalling Immune Compass removes its private database, files, and the encryption key held in Android's secure key storage. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so there is no device-backup copy to clear afterwards.

What deletion does not reach

An export or visit document you have already saved or shared is outside the app and must be deleted wherever it now lives — for example in your files, email or a messaging app.

What we hold

Nothing about you. There is no account, no cloud database, no analytics and no crash reporting, so there is no copy of your health record for us to delete.
