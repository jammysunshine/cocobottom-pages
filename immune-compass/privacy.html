Immune Compass — Privacy notice

Effective 19 October 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Immune Compass is a personal health record for people living with autoimmune conditions. You use it to log symptoms and check-ins, medications, lab results, photographs, documents and reminders, and to prepare visit packs for your appointments. It keeps all of that on your device. There is no Immune Compass account, no sign-in, no cloud database, and no automatic upload.

Not a medical device

Immune Compass is a record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition. It does not give medical advice, and nothing it shows replaces advice from a qualified clinician. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Stored on your device

Your profile and conditions, symptoms and check-ins, medications, lab results, notes, photographs, documents, reminders, visit packs and exports are written to the app's private storage on your device. Record content and attached files are encrypted with a key held in Android's secure key storage on that device. Content leaves the app only when you choose to export or share it.

What we do not collect

We do not receive your health records, photographs, documents or notes. The app contains no analytics service, no crash-reporting service and no advertising, and it does not use the Android advertising ID. We do not sell or share your data, because we never have it.

Text recognition and drafting

When you ask the app to read a photographed lab report or a document, the text is recognised on your device using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. Your images and the recognised text are not sent anywhere. Google's ML Kit library may send Google limited technical information about the library itself — such as device model, Android and app version, and performance metrics — under Google's own policies. It does not receive your images, document text or health records.

This release has no cloud AI service. Any drafting or summarising feature in the app runs on your device, and your text, photographs and documents are not sent to a server to produce it.

Purchases

This release offers no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Permissions

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a health record. Photographs and documents are read only when you choose them through Android's own picker. Notifications are used only for reminders you set; they are scheduled on the device, with no push-notification server. The app does not request microphone access. Sharing happens only when you ask to export or share. You can change any of these in Android Settings.

Device backup

Android's own backup service is switched off for this app, so your health records stay in the app's private storage rather than being copied into a Google account backup. That also means that uninstalling the app, or losing or resetting the device, takes unexported content with it — export what you want to keep.

Retention, export, and deletion

Your content stays on the device until you erase it in the app or remove the app. The app can export your records as a file you then save or share through Android's share sheet, and can prepare a visit document. In-app erase removes your saved workspace from the device after a confirmation. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its private data. A file you have exported or shared is outside the app and must be deleted wherever you put it. See the deletion page.

Security

Content is held in application-private storage, encrypted as described above, with Android's own platform protections. No system is perfectly secure, and device loss, app removal or storage failure can make unexported content unrecoverable.

Children

Immune Compass is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Changes

If a future version adds any online service — for example cloud processing, purchases, or diagnostics — this page and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is released, and any such service will ask for your consent first.
