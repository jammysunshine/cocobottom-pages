Immune Compass — Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Immune Compass is published by Coco Bottom, the developer named on its Google Play listing. Questions about this policy or your data go to our privacy contact, cocobottom2012@gmail.com.

Immune Compass is a personal health record for people living with autoimmune conditions. You use it to log symptoms and check-ins, medications, lab results, photographs, documents and reminders, and to prepare visit packs for your appointments. It keeps all of that on your device. There is no Immune Compass account, no sign-in, no cloud database, and no automatic upload.

Not a medical device

Immune Compass is a record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition. Where it shows trends or patterns in your entries, those are summaries of what you logged, not a diagnosis, an assessment or a treatment recommendation. It does not give medical advice, and nothing it shows replaces advice from a qualified clinician. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Stored on your device

Your profile and conditions, symptoms and check-ins, medications, lab results, notes, photographs, documents, reminders, visit packs and exports are written to the app's private storage on your device. Record content and attached files are encrypted with a key held in Android's secure key storage on that device. Content leaves the app only when you choose to share or save a file.

What we collect

We, Coco Bottom, do not receive your health records, photographs, documents, notes or any other content you enter. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own and no advertising, and it does not use the Android advertising ID. We do not sell or share your data.

One component sends data to Google, described in the next section. Google Play's Data safety section for this app declares it as Diagnostics and Device or other IDs, collected for analytics.

Text recognition (Google ML Kit)

When you ask the app to read a photographed lab report or a document, the text is recognised on your device using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. Your images and the recognised text are not sent anywhere.

The ML Kit library itself sends Google information about its own operation, which Google says it uses for diagnostics and usage analytics of ML Kit. According to Google's ML Kit data disclosure, this is:

  • device information, such as manufacturer, model and Android version;
  • app information, such as the app's package name and version;
  • a per-installation identifier for this installation of the app;
  • performance metrics, API configuration and error codes.

It is sent over an encrypted connection and handled under Google's own policies. It does not include your images, document text or health records, and it never reaches us. You cannot turn it off inside the app.

Drafting

This release has no cloud AI service and no AI model. Any drafting or summarising feature runs on your device, and your text, photographs and documents are not sent to a server to produce it.

Purchases

This release has no billing service: it offers no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Permissions

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a health record. Photographs and documents are read only when you choose them through Android's own picker. Notifications are used only for reminders you set; they are scheduled on the device, with no push-notification server. The app does not request microphone access. Sharing happens only when you ask to share or save a file. You can change any of these in Android Settings.

Device backup and moving phones

Android's cloud backup is switched off for this app. Neither Android backup nor phone-to-phone transfer copies your health records or the encryption key, so your record stays in the app's private storage on this device. Uninstalling the app, or losing, resetting or replacing the device, leaves the record behind and it cannot be recovered.

The app's workspace backup file is encrypted with this installation's key. It can be restored only by the same installation on the same device. It cannot be opened on another phone or after reinstalling, so it is not a way to keep your records. To keep a copy you can read anywhere, save or share a visit pack as a PDF.

Retention, sharing, and deletion

Your content stays on the device until you erase it in the app or remove the app. The app can prepare a visit pack, which you save as a PDF or share through Android's share sheet. In-app erase removes the current workspace's records and private files from the device after a confirmation. App settings and the device encryption key remain until you uninstall. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its private data, including the key. A file you have saved or shared is outside the app and must be deleted wherever you put it. See the deletion page.

Security

Content is held in application-private storage, encrypted as described above, with Android's own platform protections. No system is perfectly secure, and device loss, app removal or storage failure can make your content unrecoverable.

Children

Immune Compass is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Changes

If a future version adds any further online service, such as cloud processing, purchases or crash reporting, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is released. Any such service that would handle your content will ask for your consent first.
