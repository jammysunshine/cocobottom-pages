Inventory Countdown — Support

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Keep your ledger safe

Inventory Countdown stores every item, quantity and recorded change on this device only, and Android's own backup is deliberately switched off for it. There is no account to restore from, and support cannot see or recover your records. Export the catalogue you need before you remove the app, replace the device, or reset it.

"Your local stock ledger is unavailable"

The app refuses to guess when it cannot open its own database, so it shows this message with a Retry local storage button rather than starting empty. Try the retry, then reopen the app. Import and export stay unavailable until the catalogue has loaded successfully — that is deliberate, so a half-read ledger is never used as the basis for a write.

Camera or photo access is blocked

The camera is only used by Scan, and only to read a barcode. If camera access is off, the screen says so and offers Allow camera while Android still permits asking; if Android will not ask again, open Android Settings, find Inventory Countdown, and allow Camera there. Item photos come from the Android system photo picker, opened by Choose photo or Replace photo on an item, and it hands the app only the picture you select. You can always type a barcode or SKU by hand and keep an item without a photo.

A count reminder did not fire

Reminders are ordinary Android notifications scheduled on this device, on the "Inventory attention" channel. Check that notifications are allowed for Inventory Countdown in Android Settings and that battery optimisation is not restricting the app. One-time reminders are available on Free; weekly and monthly cadences are Pro.

Export or import a CSV

Open Data, then use Export CSV — the file has one row per active item, with name, SKU, barcode, unit, quantity, reorder threshold, supplier name, supplier contact reference, ordering note, lead-time note, reference cost, currency and status, and is handed to the Android share sheet. Choose CSV to preview validates every row and shows you what will be created, updated, restored or skipped before Apply staged import writes anything as one all-or-nothing change; Cancel preview throws the staged plan away. Both import and export are Pro features.

Delete your data

Settings & privacy has Delete everything, which permanently removes every item, recorded change, supplier note, count list, count session, reminder and item photo after a confirmation. Export first if you need a record. See the deletion page.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Prices and billing come from Google Play. Use Restore purchases on the Pro screen while signed into the Play account that made the purchase. See the purchase help page.
