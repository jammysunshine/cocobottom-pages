Inventory Countdown — Delete your data

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Delete one item

Open the item from your catalogue and tap Delete item at the bottom of the Item screen. The app asks "Delete item permanently?" and warns "This removes the item and its local adjustment history. Export first if you need a record." Choose Delete to confirm or Cancel to keep it. If you only want the item out of the active catalogue but want to keep its history, use Archive item instead — an archived item can be brought back with Restore to active catalogue.

Delete everything on this device

Open Settings & privacy from the home screen and find the card Delete everything on this device. Tap Delete everything. The app asks "Delete every item and every recorded change on this device?" — confirm with Yes, delete everything, or back out with Cancel and keep my ledger.

Confirming removes every item, quantity, recorded adjustment, supplier note, count list, count session, reminder and item photo the app holds, deletes the photo files it manages, cancels scheduled count reminders, and erases your diagnostics answer. When it finishes it reports back how many items, recorded changes, reminders and item photos were removed. It cannot be undone, and because nothing was ever uploaded there is no copy to restore from. If you need a record, export first with Export CSV on the Import & export screen (a Pro feature).

Delete everything by removing the app

Uninstalling Inventory Countdown removes its private database file and its private photo and export files. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so there is no device-backup copy to clear afterwards.

Turn off diagnostics

Diagnostics are off unless you turned on Send anonymous diagnostics in Settings & privacy. Switching it off stops any further sending immediately — the app detaches the analytics adapter and closes the crash client before it even records your new answer. The switch then reads "Off — nothing is sent."

What deletion does not reach

A CSV you have already exported and shared is outside the app and must be deleted wherever you sent it. Deleting local data does not remove a Pro purchase: it belongs to your Google Play account, not to this ledger, which is why the app deliberately keeps the local record of that entitlement when it wipes everything else. Any scrubbed operational warning already sent to Sentry while diagnostics were on is covered below, not by the in-app delete.

What we hold

There is no account and no server copy of your stock records, so there is nothing on our side for us to delete. RevenueCat holds the anonymous purchase entitlement for your install. If you turned diagnostics on, Sentry may hold warning events that contain only an operation code from a fixed list plus a release, environment and event id — no item names, quantities, suppliers, barcodes, file names or anything identifying you or your device. No analytics destination is configured in this release, so there are no product-analytics events to hold.
