Inventory Countdown — Purchase help

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Purchases belong to your Google account

There is no Inventory Countdown account. Paid access is attached to the Google Play account that made the purchase, on this device.

The one product

Inventory Countdown Pro is a one-time purchase. The Inventory Countdown Pro screen states it plainly: "A one-time purchase. There is no subscription.", and the price line reads "paid once". There is no subscription, no renewal, no trial and no billing period to manage. The app shows the product title and localised price supplied by Google Play; the Play purchase confirmation is authoritative for tax and the final charge.

What Pro adds

  • Unlimited active items
  • CSV import
  • CSV export
  • History for archived items
  • Weekly and monthly count reminders

What Free keeps, always

Free keeps 30 active items, and nothing you have already recorded is ever removed or locked by reaching that limit. Free also keeps editing, counting, archiving and deleting anything already recorded, history for active items, one-time count reminders, barcode scanning and item photos.

Buy

Open Pro from the home screen and tap Buy Pro for the shown price. If the store cannot be reached, the screen says so — "The store cannot be reached from this build" — and nothing is offered for sale; your ledger keeps working exactly as before.

Restore access

  1. Check the device is signed into the same Google account that made the purchase.
  2. Open the Pro screen.
  3. Tap Restore purchases.
  4. Stay online until the result appears.

Restoring never creates a charge, and it works even when the store offering cannot be loaded. Family sharing depends on how the Play product is configured and is not promised unless the Play listing says so.

Pending, refunded, or revoked

A pending Play transaction unlocks nothing until Play confirms it. Closing the purchase sheet creates no entitlement. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, the Pro features stop after the next entitlement check — but every item and every recorded change already on the device stays, stays readable, and stays editable.

Nothing to cancel

Because this is a one-time purchase, there is no subscription to cancel and nothing that renews. Uninstalling the app or using Delete everything does not revoke the purchase: it belongs to your store account, not to the ledger on this phone, so reinstalling and tapping Restore purchases brings Pro back.

Charges and refunds

Google processes payment and refund requests under its own policies. We cannot see your full card information and cannot reverse a Play charge directly. Check your Play purchase history before contacting support.
