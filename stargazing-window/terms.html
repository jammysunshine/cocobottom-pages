Stargazing Window — Terms of use

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

What this app is

Stargazing Window ranks the coming nights' dark-sky observing windows for a place you choose, using Sun and Moon geometry calculated on your device plus an optional cloud-cover forecast from Open-Meteo, and lets you keep a plan with notes, a gear checklist, a local reminder, and a PDF export. You must be legally able to accept these terms and use the app only where permitted.

Planning, not safety advice

A ranked window is a statement about darkness, moon, and forecast cloud. It is not road, wildlife, access, emergency, travel, or personal-safety advice, and it is not a weather warning, a land-access permission, an aviation or marine forecast, or a substitute for official emergency information. The gear checklist is your own reminder list, not a safety certification. Forecasts are predictions and are frequently wrong; astronomical calculations are approximations and depend on the coordinates, elevation, and time zone you supply. You are responsible for where you go, when you go, who you tell, and what you take.

Your content

You keep all rights in the places, targets, notes, checklists, and plans you create. They stay on your device. No licence in that content is granted to the developer — there is no server to which it could be sent. Only a coordinate rounded to roughly one kilometre is transmitted, and only when you request a forecast.

Third-party forecast data

Cloud-cover forecasts come from Open-Meteo and are supplied on their terms, not ours. Availability, accuracy, resolution, rate limits, and continued operation of that service are outside our control, and the app is designed to keep working — without cloud data — when it is unavailable.

Local data is your responsibility

There is no account and no developer-operated backup, and Android's own backup is deliberately switched off for this app. Keeping the device, preserving access to it, and exporting the plans you must retain are yours to manage. Deletion in the app, and uninstalling the app, are irreversible.

Free and paid access

This release is free and sells nothing.
