Stargazing Window — Purchase help

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Nothing is for sale in this release

Stargazing Window release 1.0 sells nothing. There is no in-app product, no price, no purchase button, no subscription, and no trial. You cannot be charged by this app. The app says the same thing on its own Pro screen, under the heading NOT AVAILABLE IN THIS BUILD: "There is no store product, purchase SDK, receipt validation, restore path, pricing, or entitlement configuration. A purchase button would be misleading, so none is shown."

Then what is the "Pro" screen?

It is an explanation of where the free tier ends, and nothing more. It appears when you tap "Plan a date beyond 7 days", when you try to save a second plan, and from the Settings screen's "Custom weights (Pro)" and "View Pro boundary" buttons. Under the heading PRO WOULD INCLUDE it lists, in the app's own words, "Full-year planning · unlimited saved plans · custom criteria · reusable gear templates". Its only two buttons are "Keep using the free 7-day outlook" and "Return to saved plans" — neither one starts a purchase. Nothing on that screen takes payment details or contacts a store.

What the free app gives you

The free tier ranks observing windows for the next 7 days and keeps one saved plan. That plan stays fully usable: you can read it, rename its checklist rows, edit its notes, schedule a local reminder, export it as a PDF, compare it against a later forecast, and delete it. Nothing about it is watermarked, locked, or time-limited.

There is nothing to restore

Because no purchase can be made, there is no entitlement attached to your Google account for this app and no "Restore purchases" control anywhere in it. If you are looking for one, it is not missing — it does not exist in this release.

There is nothing to cancel

There is no subscription and no recurring charge. Uninstalling the app ends nothing billable, because nothing billable was ever started. If you see a charge in your Google Play purchase history that names Stargazing Window, it did not come from this release — check the purchase in Google Play (profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Budget & history) and contact us at the address below with what it says.

If paid features arrive later

If a paid tier is ever released, it would be sold through Google Play, which would supply the product title, the localised price, the billing terms, and the purchase confirmation before you paid. This page and the Play listing would be updated first. Until then, treat any offer to sell you Stargazing Window features outside Google Play as not ours.
