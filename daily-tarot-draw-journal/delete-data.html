Daily Tarot Draw Journal — Delete your data

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Delete one reflection

Open the entry from your journal list and choose Delete entry. That removes the draw, its reflection text, and its date from this device.

Delete all local data

Settings has a control that permanently removes the app's database and private files after a confirmation. It cannot be undone. Export anything you want to keep first.

Delete everything by removing the app

Uninstalling the app removes its private database and files. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so there is no device-backup copy to clear afterwards.

Turn off analytics and diagnostics

Both are off unless you switched them on. Switching them off in Settings stops any further collection.

What deletion does not reach

A reflection you have already shared is outside the app and must be deleted wherever it now lives. Deleting local data does not reverse a Google Play purchase.

What we hold

There is no account and no cloud journal, so there is no copy of your reflections for us to delete. RevenueCat holds the anonymous purchase entitlement for your install. If you opted in, PostHog may hold allowlisted analytics events and Sentry may hold scrubbed crash diagnostics, neither containing journal content.
