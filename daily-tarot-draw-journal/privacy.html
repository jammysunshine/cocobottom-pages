Daily Tarot Draw Journal — Privacy notice

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Daily Tarot Draw Journal records a daily card draw and the written reflection you attach to it. Everything you write stays on your device. There is no account, no cloud journal, and no automatic upload.

Stored on your device

Your draws, reflection text, entry dates, deck selection, search history within the journal, and any reminder times you set are written to the app's private database. Content leaves the app only when you choose to share or export a reflection.

Optional analytics and diagnostics

Analytics and crash reporting are off until you switch them on in Settings. When on, PostHog may receive allowlisted event names, counts, and technical device and app information, and Sentry may receive scrubbed crash diagnostics. The app forbids sending reflection text, card meanings, entry titles, search terms, dates of individual entries, filenames, or file paths through those adapters. You can withdraw consent in Settings at any time, which stops any further collection.

Purchases

Google Play processes payment. RevenueCat receives an anonymous installation identifier and the store transaction and entitlement information the app needs to show the product, unlock access, and restore purchases. The app never receives your payment-card details.

Reminders

If you enable a daily reminder, the notification is scheduled by Android on your device. No reminder time or notification content is sent anywhere.

Permissions

Notification permission is requested only if you choose to set a reminder, and the app works fully without it. Sharing happens only when you ask to export or share a reflection. You can change these in Android Settings.

Device backup

Android's own backup service is switched off for this app, so your journal stays in the app sandbox rather than being copied into a Google account backup. That also means a lost or reset device takes unexported reflections with it — export what you want to keep.

Retention, export, and deletion

Entries stay until you delete them, delete all local data, or remove the app. Reflections can be exported and shared individually. Deleting local data does not cancel a Google Play purchase, and does not erase records that Google, RevenueCat, PostHog, or Sentry retain under their own policies.

Security

Journal content uses application-private storage and Android's own platform protections. No system is perfectly secure, and device loss, app removal, or storage failure can make unexported reflections unrecoverable.

Entertainment, not advice

The app is for personal reflection and entertainment. It does not provide medical, psychological, financial, legal, or other professional advice.

Children and international processing

The app is not directed to children. Google, RevenueCat, PostHog, and Sentry may process data in countries other than yours under their published safeguards.
