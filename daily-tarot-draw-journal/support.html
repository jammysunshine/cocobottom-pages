Daily Tarot Draw Journal — Support

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Keep your work safe

The app stores your journal on this device, and Android's own backup is deliberately switched off for it. There is no account to restore from. Export the reflections that matter before you remove the app, replace the device, or reset it.

A reminder is not arriving

Open Android Settings, find the app, and allow Notifications. Battery optimisation and Do Not Disturb can also delay or suppress a scheduled reminder. The app works fully without notification permission — you just have to open it yourself.

An entry will not save

The free tier retains a limited number of reflections. When you reach it the app tells you on screen and keeps your draft exactly as typed — buy Pro to save it, or delete an older entry first. Nothing you typed is discarded without telling you.

Reflections after paid access ends

Everything already written stays readable. Losing the entitlement never deletes journal records.

Export or delete your data

Reflections can be exported and shared individually, and Settings can permanently delete all local app data after a confirmation. See the deletion page.

Purchases

Prices and billing come from Google Play. Use "Restore purchases" while signed into the Play account that made the purchase. See the purchase help page.
