What this app is

Colour Match Board is a local workspace for keeping track of the paint, tile, fabric, furniture and craft-material colours you are choosing between on a real project: you record them, compare them, and mark one chosen and the rest rejected. You must be legally able to accept these terms and use the app only where permitted.

A record of your choice, not a colour standard

The app stores the hex values you type and draws them on your screen. A screen rendering is only an approximation of a physical material, and it varies with your display, its calibration, and the light you are standing in. Colour Match Board is not a colorimeter, not a colour-matching service, and is not affiliated with any paint or material manufacturer. Do not rely on it to confirm a shade, a batch, or a match before ordering — check a physical sample. You are responsible for what you record, what you choose, and what you buy.
