Colour Match Board — Purchase help

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

What is offered

There is one product: Colour Match Board Pro. It is a one-time purchase — there is no subscription and nothing renews. The Pro screen shows the price supplied by Google Play and labels it paid once. Open it from the board with See what Pro adds.

What Free keeps

The free tier keeps 1 active project and 12 swatches in it. Anything past that limit is held as a locked draft rather than thrown away, so nothing you entered is lost if you never buy. Entering, comparing, editing and deleting colours keep working either way.

If nothing is for sale on your device

If Google Play returns no product, the Pro screen says The store cannot be reached from this build and offers nothing to buy. That is not an error you need to fix: every project and swatch you already have stays on the device and keeps working. Check that you are online and signed into Google Play, then reopen the screen. Restore purchases still works in that state if you bought Pro previously.

Buying

On the Pro screen tap Buy Pro for {price} — it reads Working… while the store is contacted. Google Play takes the payment. A pending Play transaction unlocks nothing until Play confirms it, and closing the purchase sheet creates no entitlement and no charge. The Play purchase confirmation is authoritative for the price, taxes and the final charge.

Restore access

  1. Check the device is signed into the same Google account that made the purchase.
  2. Open the board and tap See what Pro adds.
  3. Tap Restore purchases.
  4. Stay online until the result appears.

Restoring never creates a charge. Pro is attached to your Google Play account, not to the board, so it survives deleting your data, uninstalling, or moving to a new device signed into the same account. Family sharing depends on how the Play product is configured and is not promised unless the Play listing says so.

There is nothing to cancel

Because Pro is a one-time purchase and not a subscription, there is no billing period, no renewal, and nothing to cancel. Uninstalling the app does not create or cancel any charge.

Refunds

Google processes payment and refund requests under its own policies. We cannot see your card information and cannot reverse a Play charge directly. Check your Google Play purchase history before contacting support.
