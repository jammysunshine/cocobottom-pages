Colour Match Board — Support

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Keep your work safe

Colour Match Board stores your boards on this device only, and Android's own backup is deliberately switched off for it. There is no account to restore from, and support cannot see or recover your boards. Write down anywhere else the hex codes you cannot afford to lose before you remove the app, replace the device, or reset it.

Colours are entered by hand

This release has no camera, photo picker or colour sampling. You type the hex code of each paint, tile, fabric or material you are considering, and add a name, note, tags and lighting context. The app asks for no camera or photo permission, so there is nothing to grant in Android Settings.

The colour on screen looks wrong

A screen can only approximate a physical material, and the same swatch will read differently under daylight, shop lighting and a warm bulb. Use the lighting note on a swatch to record where you saw it, and confirm against a real sample before you buy.

I have hit the free limit

Free keeps 1 active project and 12 swatches in it. Anything past that is held as a locked draft rather than deleted, so nothing you entered is thrown away. Tap See what Pro adds on the board to see the one-time purchase. See the purchase help page.

The Pro screen says the store cannot be reached

That means Google Play returned no product to this build. Check you are online and signed into Play, then reopen the screen. Everything already on the board keeps working, and Restore purchases still works if you bought Pro before.

Diagnostics

Send anonymous diagnostics in Settings & privacy is off unless you turn it on. In this release no analytics destination is configured, so it sends no analytics events either way, and no screen currently reports a crash. It can never carry your project names, swatch names, colour values, notes or tags. See the privacy notice.

Delete your data

A project has a Delete control and a swatch has Remove. To clear the whole device, open Settings & privacy and use Delete everything, then confirm with Yes, delete everything. Uninstalling also removes it all. See the deletion page.
