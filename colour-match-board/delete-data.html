Colour Match Board — Delete your data

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

App package com.cocobottom.colourmatchboard. Everything this app holds is stored only on your device, so every deletion below happens on the device and takes effect immediately.

Delete one project or one swatch

On the board, a project has a Delete control. It asks Delete project? and you confirm with Delete or back out with Cancel. An individual swatch has a Remove control. Deleting a project also removes the swatches under it; deleting a swatch removes its name, hex and RGB values, note, tags and lighting context, and clears any chosen or rejected mark that referred to it. Both take effect on this device immediately.

Delete everything in the app

  1. Open Settings & privacy from the board.
  2. Find the card Delete everything on this device.
  3. Tap Delete everything.
  4. At the prompt Delete every project and swatch on this device? tap Yes, delete everything — it reads Deleting… while it works. Cancel and keep my boards backs out instead.

This permanently removes every project, swatch, note, tag and private file the app holds. It cannot be undone, and because nothing was ever uploaded there is no copy to restore from.

Delete everything by removing the app

Uninstalling Colour Match Board removes its private database and files. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so there is no device-backup copy to clear afterwards.

Turn off diagnostics

Settings & privacy has a switch labelled Send anonymous diagnostics. It is off by default and reads Off — nothing is sent. Turning it off stops any further reporting. In this release no analytics destination is configured and no screen currently reports a crash event, so leaving it on sends nothing either — but the switch is the control if that ever changes.

What deletion does not reach

Anything you copied out of the app by hand — a hex code written into a note, a message, or a shopping list — is outside the app and must be deleted wherever it now lives. Deleting your data does not undo or refund a Google Play purchase: a Pro purchase belongs to your store account, not to this board, and survives deletion and reinstall.

What we hold off your device

There is no account and no server-side copy of your boards, so there is nothing about your projects, swatches or colours for us to delete. If you opened the Pro screen or made a purchase, RevenueCat holds an anonymous installation identifier and the store entitlement for that install; Google Play holds the purchase record under its own policies. No analytics or crash records are held for this release, because no analytics destination is configured and no crash event is emitted.
