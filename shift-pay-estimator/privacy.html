Shift Pay Estimator — Privacy notice

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Shift Pay Estimator works out what a roster is likely to pay, using pay rates and shifts you enter yourself. Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account, no payroll connection, and no automatic upload.

Stored on your device

Pay profiles, hourly and penalty rates, allowances, shift times and dates, saved estimates, and comparison history are written to the app's private storage. Content leaves the app only when you choose to export or share it.

Not connected to your employer

The app has no link to any payroll system, employer, bank, or tax authority. It does not read your bank account, your payslips, or your real roster. Every figure it shows comes from what you typed.

Optional analytics and diagnostics

Analytics and crash reporting are off until you switch them on in Settings. When on, PostHog may receive allowlisted event names, counts, and technical device and app information, and Sentry may receive scrubbed crash diagnostics. The app forbids sending pay rates, amounts, employer names, profile names, shift dates, or saved estimate contents through those adapters. You can withdraw consent in Settings at any time, which stops any further collection.

Purchases

Google Play processes payment. RevenueCat receives an anonymous installation identifier and the store transaction and entitlement information the app needs to show the product, unlock access, and restore purchases. The app never receives your payment-card details.

Permissions

The app requests no camera, location, contacts, or storage permission. Sharing happens only when you ask to export an estimate.

Device backup

Android's own backup service is switched off for this app, so your profiles and estimates stay in the app sandbox rather than being copied into a Google account backup. A lost or reset device takes unexported estimates with it.

Retention, export, and deletion

Saved profiles and estimates stay until you delete them, delete all local data, or remove the app. Pro can export an estimate as CSV or PDF. Deleting local data does not cancel a Google Play purchase, and does not erase records that Google, RevenueCat, PostHog, or Sentry retain under their own policies.

Security

Your figures use application-private storage and Android's own platform protections. No system is perfectly secure, and device loss, app removal, or storage failure can make unexported estimates unrecoverable.

Estimates, not payslips

Every figure is an estimate produced from the rates you entered. It is not payroll advice and not a guarantee of what you will be paid.

Children and international processing

The app is a work tool and is not directed to children. Google, RevenueCat, PostHog, and Sentry may process data in countries other than yours under their published safeguards.
