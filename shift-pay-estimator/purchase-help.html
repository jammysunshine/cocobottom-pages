Shift Pay Estimator — Purchase help

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Purchases belong to your Google account

There is no Shift Pay Estimator account. Paid access is attached to the Google Play account that made the purchase, on this device.

Products

Pro is a one-time purchase intended to unlock unlimited saved pay profiles and estimates, profile templates, comparison history, and CSV and PDF export. Free keeps every calculation, line item and warning on screen. The app shows the product title and localised price supplied by Google Play; the Play purchase confirmation is authoritative for the taxes and the final charge. This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription — there is nothing to renew or cancel.

Restore access

  1. Check the device is signed into the same Google account that made the purchase.
  2. Open Shift Pay Estimator Settings or the unlock screen.
  3. Choose "Restore purchases".
  4. Stay online until the result appears.

Restoring never creates a charge. Family sharing depends on how the Play product is configured and is not promised unless the Play listing says so.

Pending, cancelled, expired, or refunded

A pending Play transaction unlocks nothing until Play confirms it. Closing the purchase sheet creates no entitlement. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, paid-only features stop after the next entitlement refresh. Anything you already saved locally stays readable.

Charges and refunds

Google processes payment and refund requests under its own policies. We cannot see your full card information and cannot reverse a Play charge directly. Check your Play purchase history before contacting support.
