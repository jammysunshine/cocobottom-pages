Shift Pay Estimator — Support

Effective 30 August 2026 · Release 1.0 · Android

Keep your work safe

The app stores profiles and estimates on this device, and Android's own backup is deliberately switched off for it. There is no account to restore from. Export the estimates that matter before you remove the app, replace the device, or reset it.

The total does not match my payslip

The app only knows the rates and shifts you entered. Check the pay profile against your award, enterprise agreement, or contract — particularly penalty rates, overtime thresholds, allowances, and how breaks are treated. The app shows its assumptions alongside every estimate so you can see what it applied.

A profile or estimate will not save

The free tier retains a limited number of pay profiles and saved estimates. When you reach the limit the app says so on screen and keeps your draft exactly as typed, including every rate you entered — buy Pro to save it, or delete an older one first.

Export is unavailable

CSV and PDF export are part of Pro. The free tier shows the same breakdown on screen, including the assumptions and the estimate limitation.

Estimates after paid access ends

Saved estimates stay listed and readable when paid access ends.

Export or delete your data

Settings can permanently delete all local app data after a confirmation. See the deletion page.

Purchases

Prices and billing come from Google Play. Use "Restore purchases" while signed into the Play account that made the purchase. See the purchase help page.
