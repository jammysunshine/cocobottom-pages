WonderStories privacy notice

WonderStories turns a photograph and a few details about someone you love into a personalised illustrated book. That means we handle photographs, voices, and details about children. This notice says exactly what we hold, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and how to make us delete it.

The short version. An adult creates the account and supplies everything. Children do not sign up, and we do not ask children for anything directly. Photographs and voice notes are stored privately, are used only to make the book you asked for, are never used to train artificial-intelligence models, are never sold, and are never used for advertising. You can delete any of it at any time, and deleting the account deletes the lot.

WHO WE ARE

WonderStories is published by Coco Bottom. For privacy law in Europe and the United Kingdom, Coco Bottom is the controller of the information described here. Our address for privacy questions, requests, and complaints is cocobottom2012@gmail.com, and we answer within 30 days.

WonderStories is offered worldwide. This notice is written to meet the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended, Canada's PIPEDA, and New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020. Where those laws differ, we apply the one that gives you the most protection.

THIS IS AN APP FOR ADULTS

You must be 18 or older to create a WonderStories account. WonderStories is not directed to children, has no child-facing sign-up, no chat, no social feed, no user-to-user messaging, and no advertising of any kind.

Children appear in WonderStories only because an adult chooses to make a book about them. When you give us a child's photograph, name, age, or voice, you are telling us that you are that child's parent or guardian, or that you have their parent or guardian's permission. If that is not true, do not upload it. If you believe a child's information has reached us without that permission, email us and we will delete it — no account needed, no questions asked beyond what we need to find it.

A child may of course read the finished book with you. Reading takes no account, collects nothing about the reader, and works the same whether an adult or a child turns the page.

WHY WE ARE ALLOWED TO HOLD IT

In the United Kingdom and Europe, our lawful bases are: contract, for everything needed to make and deliver the book you ordered; consent, for the photographs and voice notes, which we only ever receive because you chose to give them and which you may withdraw at any time by deleting them; legitimate interests, for keeping the service secure and for the narrow product analytics described below; and legal obligation, for the transaction records tax law requires us to keep.

Photographs of an identifiable person are treated as sensitive information under the Australian Privacy Act, and we collect them only with your consent and only for making the book.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE, AND WHERE IT GOES

Your brief, photographs, and voice notes are uploaded to private storage in Google Cloud that is scoped to your account. The storage is not public: there is no shareable address for your originals, and access is granted by short-lived, single-purpose links issued to you.

Photographs are used to generate the illustrated character you then approve. Original photographs are kept separate from the approved illustration and are never placed in the pages of a book, in a share link, or in anything a recipient receives.

WE DO NOT TRAIN AI ON YOUR FAMILY

Photographs, voice notes, names, and the details in your brief are used to make your book and nothing else. They are not used to train, fine-tune, or improve any artificial-intelligence model, ours or anyone else's, and we require the same in writing from every processor that touches them.

WHAT ANALYTICS MAY NEVER SEE

Product analytics is limited to a fixed, approved list of events and properties — which screen was reached, whether a step completed, which door was chosen. Everything else is refused by the app itself rather than by a promise.

Story text, a child's or recipient's identity, names, exact birth dates, photographs, voice, memories, relationship details, appearance choices, private answers, file links, storage locations, and raw error text never enter analytics.

PERMISSIONS THE APP ASKS FOR

Each is asked for at the moment you use the feature, and each may be refused.

  • Photos — only when you choose to import a photograph.
  • Camera — only when you choose to take one.
  • Microphone — only when you choose to record a voice note. There is a text box for every voice note, so refusing costs you nothing but the shortcut.

WonderStories does not record in the background, and does not access your photo library beyond the pictures you pick.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

  • Photographs and voice notes: deleted 30 days after the last book that used them was made, unless you have chosen to keep the character on your shelf for a future book. Delete one yourself and it goes immediately.
  • Your brief and your books: kept while your account exists, because they are what your shelf is.
  • Purchase and tax records: kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and no longer. These survive account deletion because the law requires it; they contain the transaction, not your child's photograph.
  • Analytics events: kept in a form that cannot identify a child, and deleted on request.

Deleting something marks it unavailable at once, withdraws any access link, and queues its permanent removal from our storage and from any processor holding a copy.

YOUR RIGHTS

Wherever you live, you may ask us to: show you what we hold; correct it; delete it; give you a copy in a portable form; restrict or object to what we do with it; and withdraw consent for the photographs and voice notes. Exercising any of them costs nothing and we will not treat you differently for it.

Most of it you can do yourself in the app, in Account. For anything else, email cocobottom2012@gmail.com. Step-by-step instructions are on the delete your data page.

If we get it wrong, complain to us first and we will answer. If our answer does not satisfy you, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au), to your national data-protection authority in the European Union, to the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom (ico.org.uk), or to the equivalent regulator where you live.

SHARING A BOOK

If you share a finished book, the person you share it with can read that book — its pages and its story. They never receive the original photographs, the brief, your email address, or anything about your account. You can revoke a share at any time.

Being straight with you about a limit: the page images inside a book you have already shared are addressed by links that do not currently expire, so a recipient who saved a direct link to a page picture may still be able to open that picture after you revoke the share. Revoking stops the book being opened, and we are making those page links expire as well. Until then, share with people you trust, as you would a printed book.

KEEPING IT SAFE

Everything travels encrypted and is stored in private, account-scoped storage. Access is granted by short-lived links issued for one purpose, our staff do not browse customer photographs, and no part of the service is designed to let one account see another's. No system is perfect; if a breach ever puts you at risk we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires, and we will tell you what to do about it.

CHANGES TO THIS NOTICE

If we change how we handle your information we will change the effective date at the top and, for anything material, tell you in the app before it takes effect. We will never make a change that applies retrospectively to information you gave us under an earlier version without asking you first.
