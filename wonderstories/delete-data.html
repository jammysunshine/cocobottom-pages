Delete your data

Everything you gave WonderStories can be removed, by you, from inside the app. Nothing here requires you to email us or wait — but if you would rather we did it, we will.

DELETE ONE PHOTOGRAPH OR VOICE NOTE

  1. Open Account → Your uploads.
  2. Choose the photograph or recording.
  3. Tap Delete.

It stops being reachable immediately, any link to it is withdrawn, and it is erased from our storage and from any service that held a copy. Books already made keep their illustrations — the illustration is not the photograph.

DELETE A CHARACTER YOU SAVED

  1. Open Account → Saved characters.
  2. Choose the character and tap Delete.

Deleting a saved character also removes the photograph it was made from, unless you have kept that photograph elsewhere.

DELETE A BOOK

  1. Open the book on your shelf.
  2. Tap … → Delete this book.

The book, its pages, and any share link go. If you have bought a printed copy, the record of that purchase stays for as long as tax law requires — that record is the transaction, not the story or the photographs.

STOP A SHARE

  1. Open the book, then … → Shared with.
  2. Tap Revoke.

The link stops opening the book. Being straight with you: the page pictures inside a book you already shared are addressed by links that do not currently expire, so someone who saved a direct link to a picture may still be able to open that picture. We are changing that. Until then, share as you would lend a printed book.

DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT AND EVERYTHING IN IT

  1. Open Account → Delete my account.
  2. Confirm.

This deletes your sign-in, your email address, every brief, every photograph, every voice note, every saved character, every book, every share, and your analytics events. It cannot be undone, and we cannot bring any of it back afterwards.

Two things survive, and only these: the record of what you purchased and when, because tax and accounting law requires us to keep it; and, if a printed book is still on its way to you, the delivery details needed to finish sending it. Neither contains a photograph, a voice note, or a child's story.

ASK US TO DO IT INSTEAD

Email cocobottom2012@gmail.com from the address on the account and say what you want removed — one photograph, one book, or all of it. We act within 30 days and confirm when it is done. There is no charge, and asking costs you nothing else.

If a child's information reached us without a parent's or guardian's permission, email us. You do not need an account, and we will find and delete it.
