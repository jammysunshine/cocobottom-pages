Delete your data

Everything you gave WonderStories can be removed, by you, from inside the app. Nothing below needs an email or a wait — but if you would rather we did it, we will.

DELETE THE PHOTOGRAPHS YOU UPLOADED

  1. Open the character screen where you added them — Create → Get to know them.
  2. Tap Delete private character photos.

They stop being reachable immediately, any link to them is withdrawn, and they are erased from our storage and from any service that held a copy. A book already made keeps its illustrations — an illustration is not the photograph it was drawn from.

DELETE A VOICE NOTE

  1. Open the same screen and find the recording.
  2. Tap Delete on the recorder.

There is a text box beside every recorder, so you never have to record anything in the first place.

WITHDRAW CONSENT FOR PHOTOS AND VOICE

On the same screen you can revoke the permission you gave for private photos and voice notes. Revoking stops any new upload at once. Uploads already made stay until you delete them, which you can do from the same place.

Changing the main character of a story deletes that character's previous private uploads as part of the change.

STOP A SHARE

  1. Open the book, then Share this keepsake privately.
  2. Tap Revoke this link.

A share link expires by itself 30 days after you create it, and revoking ends it sooner. The link never reveals where anything is stored, and a recipient never receives your photographs, your brief, or your email address.

Being straight with you about one limit: the page pictures inside a book that has already been opened are addressed by links that do not currently expire, so someone who saved a direct link to a picture may still be able to open that picture after you revoke the share. Revoking stops the book being opened. We are making those page links expire too. Until then, share as you would lend a printed book.

DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT AND EVERYTHING IN IT

  1. Open Me → Delete your WonderStories account.
  2. Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

The account stops accepting new stories, purchases and uploads immediately, every active share link is revoked, and every private upload is queued for permanent deletion. The app tells you how many links were revoked and how many uploads were queued, so you are not asked to take it on trust. It cannot be undone from the app.

Two things survive, and only these: the record of what you purchased and when, because tax and accounting law requires us to keep it; and, if a printed book is still on its way to you, the delivery details needed to finish sending it. Neither contains a photograph, a voice note, or a child's story.

ASK US TO DO IT INSTEAD

Email cocobottom2012@gmail.com from the address on the account and say what you want removed — one photograph, one story, or all of it. We act within 30 days and confirm when it is done. There is no charge.

If a child's information reached us without a parent's or guardian's permission, email us. You do not need an account, and we will find it and delete it.
