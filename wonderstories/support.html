Support

A real person reads every message. Write to cocobottom2012@gmail.com and you will hear back within two business days.

Include the email address on your account and, for an order, the order number from your receipt. It saves a round trip and gets your book to you faster.

ORDERS AND PRINTED BOOKS

Where is my printed book?

Printed books are made to order. Printing takes a few days, then delivery time depends on where you are. You will get a dispatch email with tracking when it leaves the printer. If it has been more than three weeks, email us and we will chase it.

It arrived damaged, or the printing is wrong

Send us a photograph of the problem and your order number. We reprint and re-send it, at no cost to you, and you do not need to return the damaged copy.

I made a mistake in the book

You approve a proof before you pay, and after approval the file goes to the printer. If you spot the mistake before printing begins, email us immediately and we will try to stop it. After it has printed we cannot undo it, but tell us anyway — if the mistake was ours, we will fix it.

PAYMENTS AND REFUNDS

Digital purchases go through Google Play. Refunds for those are handled by Google: open Play Store → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, find the order, and request a refund. If Google declines and you think it was our fault, email us.

Printed books are paid for separately. Email us for a refund on one of those.

Nothing here reduces the rights you have under consumer law where you live — including the Australian Consumer Law, which entitles you to a repair, replacement, or refund for goods that are faulty or not as described.

YOUR ACCOUNT

I cannot sign in

Sign in with the same method you signed up with — an email link, or the social account you used. If you no longer have access to that address, email us from any address and tell us the old one; we will verify what we can and help.

My purchase did not appear on a new phone

Sign in with the same account you bought it on and your shelf comes back with it — purchases are held against your account, not your handset. If a book is still missing, send us the order number and we will restore it.

I want my data deleted

You can do all of it yourself — see delete your data — or email us and we will do it for you.

PRIVACY, CHILDREN, AND SAFETY

What we hold and why is set out in the privacy notice. WonderStories is an app for adults; children do not have accounts and we never collect anything from a child directly.

If you believe a child's photograph or details reached us without a parent's or guardian's permission, email us. You do not need an account and we will find and delete it.
